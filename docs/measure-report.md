The pinned 1F3D9 snapshot contains 391,957 usable exported events. Its report records acting share 0.288, loop rate 0.623, cross-author share 0.0511, 973 distinct author pairs, divergence 0.588, persistence 0.849, and 110 adopted novel kinds. These values describe that export under S11; they do not establish comparable capabilities or causality between systems. The adapters follow actual source schemas: Emergence JSON/zipped JSON; 1F3D9 fingerprint/record NDJSON envelopes; MoltNet posts/comments Parquet with parent joins; MediaWiki history TSV or a named-field projection; and native S7 compressed event logs. MediaWiki's actual `MediawikiEvent.toTSVLine` emits 78 columns, excluding `page_artificial_id`; its HQL table includes that extra internal field. The adapter pins the emitted order and rejects mismatched widths. MoltNet’s four original Parquet tables were downloaded in full, pinned to commit `fbb34f7eda1981b279361284aa2ba656b645765a` and SHA-256 checked: 1,043,908,724 compressed bytes. The derived fully-connected posts table was not combined with the originals, which would duplicate records. Source-schema fixtures remain separate from the full-release measurement.

The Wikipedia workflow supports an enumerated complete eligible frame and a preregistered exact uniform rejection draw from the complete pinned category universe. The latter preserves every draw/rejection and does not claim the 40 selected worlds enumerate the eligible frame. It filters 500–5000 articles, uses 2024 edits and aligned seven-day intervals, computes Spearman correlations of changes, and bootstraps whole worlds 1000 times for 95% intervals. The final two days of 2024 form a short final interval; zero pageviews leave a logarithmic change undefined rather than adding a pseudocount. Missing/incomplete frames are reported unavailable. For the actual acquisition, the complete 20261101 category SQL table contains 2,653,947 candidate categories; every accepted tree is traversed through depth 2 with full API pagination. See `wikipedia-acquisition.md` for acquisition choices, source limits and replay proofs.

The actual uniform draw has now completed: **798 distinct candidate draws yielded 40 eligible trees containing 48,562 distinct article IDs**. The independent proof replay checked 7,107 durable API response bodies and the complete pinned SQL/SQLite universe. Every rejection and accepted closure is retained in `.cache/measure-wikipedia/sample-proof.json` and its referenced source transcript. This establishes the sample, not an enumerated eligible population. All twelve official 2024 monthly history files have been downloaded and verified, totaling **7,432,569,766 compressed bytes**. Filtering all 82,243,058 rows retained 457,885 rows for the selected distinct articles. Historical title alignment is complete. The serial reader stopped after 18,540 article projections, leaving 30,022 uncollected, once actual source evidence proved all 2,120 weekly sums unavailable. No complete annual readership download is claimed.

The independent history-only pass is complete in `.cache/measure/results/wikipedia-histories/validation.json`, with normal per-world JSON reports and weekly CSVs. Across the 40 worlds, including their actual article overlaps, 462,624 source rows yield **384,325 attributable events**; 78,299 rows lack a usable author. Every world has all **53 seven-day periods intersecting 2024**, including its final two-day period. The first pass exposed a missing trailing empty period in one world; the adapter now supplies the already-supported known end boundary to the frozen core. No event, quantity or formula was added. That initial pass remains in `.cache/measure-acquisition/wiki-history-first-pass.json`. That stage used no readership input and computed no correlations; the final actual validation reused the same history-measure routine.

Readership normalization checks stable-ID existence even for complete positive title series. The history projection has 39,529 single-valued source creation records and 9,033 unknowns. These are existence-by evidence, not lifetime births: nine actual January 1 ID/title observations precede a later creation record. Another 3,444 unknown IDs gain a January 2 existence bound from that retained official dump, leaving 5,589 without either source. Positive counts before an existence bound remain NA. The official mature-data contract decodes omitted daily items and 404s as published zero; those zeros attribute no predecessor traffic and need no existence proof. Unknown/moved/suppressed titles and actual request failures remain unavailable. This corrects the earlier blanket missing→NA and precreation→zero interpretations without changing source bodies, the sample or any frozen formula. Current and historical evidence, raw omission diagnostics and the serial-reader restart boundary are retained in [the acquisition record](wikipedia-acquisition.md). The final audit independently verified all 2,120 weekly obstructions against 92 actual stable IDs and original AQS response bodies. No NA was inferred merely because an article was uncollected.

The **full MoltNet release** report is `.cache/measure/results/moltnet-full/report.json`. Its 1,044,455 posts and 3,161,324 comments yield **4,205,367 attributable events** from 149,568 distinct actors, of whom 13,773 meet the aggregate activity threshold. Exactly 412 rows lack an author;19 rows lack a timestamp (all 19 overlap those author exclusions). There are 4,621 reply targets absent from the usable published tables. Acting share is 0, loop rate is **0.577834**, and C/CA are 0; cross-author, lookback, novelty and role measures are explicitly unavailable under the frozen social-only adapter. The full read/join/sort/measure pass took 150.525s with peak RSS 1,403,232KiB on this host. This measures the complete pinned release, not the entire platform: the public crawl has acknowledged coverage gaps. Votes are not synthesized, and the table counts are not treated as platform population totals.

Reproduce it with `python3 -m measure.fetch moltbook` and `python3 bake/run.py -m measure moltbook --input .cache/measure-sources/moltbook --output .cache/measure/results/moltnet-full`. The report embeds the exact release/file hashes, input/exclusion/orphan counts and a separate acquisition benchmark. The public inputs remain in `.cache`, under the source’s CC-BY-4.0 terms.

The synthetic offline benchmark processed 100,000 events for 100 agents in 1.395 seconds median over five repetitions; the largest observed duration was 1.629 seconds and peak RSS was 25.5 MiB. This measures the analysis package, not simulation frame performance or an external validation result. Reproduce with `python -m measure.benchmark`. Core tests cover literal ratios above one, objectless cross-author changes, seed exclusion, root adoption, bounded loop widths, period history, undefined distributions, source schemas and world-cluster bootstrap.

Reproduce the corrected comparison with `python -m measure.fetch emergence`, then `python -m measure.validate --input .cache/measure-sources/emergence/study-input.json --output .cache/emergence-validation`. The latter writes a new preregistration before loading inputs. `python -m measure.fetch onefthree` fetches the pinned public snapshot; run `python -m measure onefthree --input .cache/measure-sources/onefthree --output .cache/onefthree-validation`. Exact input URLs/hashes are in `measure/sources.json`; source data remains in `.cache` and is not redistributed. Emergence's CC-BY-NC-4.0 restriction applies to those research inputs.
